Give someone a night at ROSSO.

Pick the amount, say who it is for and pay online. The code appears instantly and is redeemed at the bar: cocktails, snacks or the whole bill. Valid for 12 months; any remaining balance stays for the next visit.

  1. Choose and pay. Credit or debit card, secure checkout with Stripe.
  2. Get the code. On screen when you finish, with a card to share or print.
  3. Redeem at the bar. Whoever receives it reads out the code and it comes off their bill.

Valid for 12 months from purchase. Not redeemable for cash. For more than 4 guests or events, see private events.
