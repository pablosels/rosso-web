Speakeasy · Roma Norte

A speakeasy in Roma Norte, behind a kitchen.

ROSSO is at Puebla 329 in Roma Norte, but you can't see it from the street. You enter through Pavorosso: cross the kitchen, pull back a red curtain, and on the other side is a 430 sq ft bar with benches around the counter, a ceiling of light circles and the DJ booth facing you.

It is a bar that explores pleasure through the senses. Red covers everything, the music is curated night by night and the menu is signature cocktails: Picarilla with tequila and chile de árbol, L'Origine du Monde with cocoa-butter gin, Camasotz with sherry and guava, and a full non-alcoholic section.

We open Tuesday to Saturday from 6 pm to 2 am and Sundays from 4 to 11 pm. Wednesday to Saturday there is a DJ session from 9 pm; on Sundays one full record plays start to finish.

Seats are few, so booking helps. Tables for up to 4 are booked right here, free of charge; larger groups and private events are arranged on WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions
Where is ROSSO?

At Puebla 329, Roma Norte, Mexico City. The entrance is through the restaurant Pavorosso: cross the kitchen and pull back the red curtain.

Do I need a reservation?

Recommended: the bar seats 32. Tables for up to 4 are booked at rossospeakeasy.com/en/reservar; groups of 5 or more, on WhatsApp at +52 56 6435 7899.

What are the opening hours?

Tuesday to Saturday 6:00 pm to 2:00 am and Sunday 4:00 pm to 11:00 pm. Closed on Mondays.

How much is a cocktail?

House cocktails run from $220 to $300 MXN and classics from $210 to $300. The full menu with current prices is at rossospeakeasy.com/en/carta.

Are there non-alcoholic options?

Yes, four house non-alcoholic cocktails: Under Wear, Copa de Edipo, El Ojo and A Coup de Crème.
