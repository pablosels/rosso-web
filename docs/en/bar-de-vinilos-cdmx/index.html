Vinyl bar · Mexico City

On Sundays one full record plays, start to finish.

On Sundays at ROSSO there is no playlist. One record is chosen and played whole, side A and side B, no skipping. From 4 to 11 pm, under red light, with the bar open.

The record of the week is announced on the Nights page, with its cover, and stays in the archive of past Sundays. Santana, The Rolling Stones, The Cure and M83 have played.

The Sunday menu adds house micheladas and clamatos to the usual cocktails. It is the day to arrive early, take a bench and stay.

To suggest a record, message us on Instagram at @rosso.speakeasy.
