Bar with DJ · Mexico City

A bar with a DJ where the booth is the first thing you see.

At ROSSO the DJ booth faces the entrance: it is the first thing you see when you pull back the curtain. Wednesday to Saturday, 9 pm to 1 am, a different selector plays each night: disco, funk, soul, house, italo, global sounds.

It is a small bar, 32 seats, with benches around the counter. The music sounds right and you can talk without shouting until the night picks up; then you dance wherever you are.

The lineup is posted every week on the Nights page, with each selector's genre and Instagram. Every DJ has a profile with the dates they have played and when they are back.

DJ nights are booked like any other: tables for up to 4 right here, groups on WhatsApp.

Frequently asked questions
Which nights have a DJ at ROSSO?

Wednesday to Saturday, 9:00 pm to 1:00 am. On Sundays a full vinyl record plays from 4:00 to 11:00 pm.

What music do they play?

It depends on the selector: disco, funk, soul, house, italo, synth pop and global sounds. The weekly lineup is at rossospeakeasy.com/en/noches.

Is there a cover charge?

No cover. Booking is recommended because the bar seats 32.

Can you dance?

Yes. The bar is small and you dance wherever you are, between the counter and the booth.
